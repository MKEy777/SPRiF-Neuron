Dear Editors,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled **“SPRiF Neurons with Spectral Memory and Scalar Reset”** for publication in **IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems**.

Spiking neural networks must retain information while communicating through spikes. Standard leaky integrate-and-fire neurons couple input integration and reset in one membrane state, allowing discharge to disturb temporal evidence.

The main contributions are summarized below.

1. We introduce SPRiF to preserve temporal evidence during spike communication. Its spectral memory combines persistence with phase-dependent temporal selectivity, while membrane reset leaves the memory coordinates unchanged at discharge.

2. We derive the temporal kernel to connect the neuron design with its temporal responses and explain how reset placement determines the direct contribution of spike history to the membrane drive.

3. Across S-MNIST, PS-MNIST, QTDB, GSC and SHD, SPRiF exceeds the compared published accuracies on four benchmarks. Matched ablations support memory preservation and the spectral basis, while delayed matching experiments demonstrate robust retention under forced spikes.

We believe this work will interest TNNLS readers studying spiking neural networks and temporal learning.
