# 表2：消融实验

**Caption:** Structural ablations of SPRiF. All variants retain five intrinsic states and eleven learned local dynamics coefficients per neuron, so that the comparison isolates the organization of those states rather than their number. Accuracy is reported as mean ± sample standard deviation over five seeds. The standard-initialization SPRiF row reuses the values reported in Table 1.

| Comparison | Variant | States / neuron | Local coefficients / neuron | PS-MNIST accuracy (%) | SHD accuracy (%) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Reference | SPRiF (standard initialization) | 5 | 11 | 95.86 ± 0.14 | 91.66 ± 0.42 |
| Structure | SPRiF with memory reset (MEMORY_RESET5) | 5 | 11 | 92.49 ± 0.36 | 87.10 ± 0.61 |
| Structure | SPRiF with three real modes (REAL_MODES3) | 5 | 11 | 93.52 ± 0.27 | 89.19 ± 0.50 |
